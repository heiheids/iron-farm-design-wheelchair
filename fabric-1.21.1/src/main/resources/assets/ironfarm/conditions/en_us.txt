How villagers spawn iron golems - full conditions (Java 1.21.1, verified)
================================================================

Author: Heishan Dashu
Homepage: https://space.bilibili.com/19428259

Applies to: Minecraft Java Edition 1.14.3 through 1.21.1 (26.x unverified)
Source: bytecode decompiled from the official 1.21.1 obfuscated jar with javap,
not paraphrased from the wiki.

NOTE: how this differs from common online sources
----------------------------------------------------------------
Several claims circulating online (including some wiki wording) are inaccurate.
Everything here follows the bytecode, and section 7 lists the differences.

Three things worth knowing up front:

  1. There is NO separate condition "no nearby villager summoned a golem in 30s"
     That comes from misreading the golem memory as a summon memory. See item 1 in section 7.

  2. The golem memory lasts 599 ticks, not 600
     Being off by one flips the result at exactly the 30 second boundary.

  3. Participants do NOT need to be panicking
     Only the villager that starts the attempt does. This directly affects farm design.


================================================================
1. Overview: two paths
================================================================

There are two completely independent paths:

  Path          How it triggers                   Group     Attempt rate
  ------------  --------------------------------  --------  ------------------
  Panic         A villager sees a hostile mob     >= 3      every 100 ticks (5 s)
  Gossip        Villagers talk (gossip present)   >= 5      on conversation

* Iron farms mostly use panic: lower threshold (3 vs 5), fixed 5 second cadence.
* Both paths share exactly the same individual conditions.


================================================================
2. Conditions every individual villager must meet
================================================================

These apply to every participant, not just the initiator.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Condition 1: must be an adult villager
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Baby villagers never sleep and never participate.

  Vanilla actually has no explicit adult check: a baby has no LAST_SLEPT memory,
  so condition 2 fails naturally. It is listed separately in the UI because
  "this is just a baby" is easier to understand than "has never slept".

----------------------------------------------------------------
Condition 2: slept within the last 20 minutes
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Window: 24000 ticks = 20 minutes = one full day cycle
  Source: ldc2_w 24000l in Villager.golemSpawnConditionsMet

  WARNING This is the easiest condition to fail.

     The villager must actually have lain down in a bed. A bed merely being
     nearby does not count.

     Farms that keep villagers caged and never let them return to a bed go dead
     after 20 minutes. This is the real reason behind many "my farm suddenly
     stopped working" reports.

  Common misconceptions:

     "once it has slept, it is fine"          -> wrong, the window keeps rolling
     "just put a bed nearby"                  -> wrong, it must actually lie down
     "name tag / boat it in place"            -> a villager in a boat cannot sleep, so this always fails

----------------------------------------------------------------
Condition 3: has not "seen" an iron golem in the last 30 seconds
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Memory: GOLEM_DETECTED_RECENTLY
  Duration: 599 ticks (NOT 600)
  Source: setMemoryWithExpiry with 599L in GolemSensor.golemDetected

  DETECTION RANGE

     A cube of 16 blocks in each axis (X/Y/Z) centred on the villager, not a sphere.
     That is 33 x 33 x 33 = 35937 blocks.
     Source: Sensor.TARGETING_RANGE = 16 combined with a bounding box inflate.

  SCAN RATE

     Once every 200 ticks (10 seconds).
     Source: the constructor argument 200 in GolemSensor.

  IMPORTANT The memory is only written if a golem is inside the cube at the
  moment the scan runs. That means:

     - a golem lingering for 10+ seconds will almost certainly be recorded
     - a golem passing through quickly may not be recorded depending on timing

  PRACTICAL IMPACT

     This is why golems must leave the spawn area quickly.

     If a golem stays within 16 blocks for more than 30 seconds, that villager
     stops spawning for the next 599 ticks, which caps your rates directly.

  NOTE This is "saw it yourself", not "someone nearby summoned one". See section 7 item 1.


================================================================
3. Group condition: participant counting
================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------
Counting rules
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Range: centred on the initiator, bounding box inflated by 10 blocks
         Source: the inflate(10, 10, 10) call in spawnGolemIfNeeded

  Slot limit: 5
         Source: Stream.limit(5) in the same method

  WARNING This limit of 5 has a very important consequence:

     The gossip threshold is also exactly 5, so ALL FIVE slots must qualify.
     One bad villager is enough to fail the check.

     The panic threshold is 3, so only 3 of the 5 slots need to qualify.

----------------------------------------------------------------
KEY FACT: participants do not need to be panicking
----------------------------------------------------------------

  This is one of the most important conclusions here, and it is widely
  misreported in guides.

  The vanilla logic is:

    1. VillagerPanicTrigger fires every 100 ticks and first counts how many
       villagers are panicking (threshold 3).
    2. Once 3 are panicking, it calls wantsToSpawnGolem on each nearby villager.
    3. wantsToSpawnGolem only checks two things:
         - LAST_SLEPT is within 24000 ticks
         - there is no GOLEM_DETECTED_RECENTLY memory
    4. Any villager meeting those two conditions participates, whether or not
       it is panicking itself.

  IMPACT ON FARM DESIGN

     You only need 3 villagers that can see the mob. Everyone else just needs
     to have slept and not seen a golem. This makes farms much easier to build.


================================================================
4. Panic path: which mobs scare villagers
================================================================

The check (VillagerPanicTrigger.canStillUse):

  1. The mob must be hostile to villagers (see the table below)
  2. It must be within the panic radius (spherical, foot centre to foot centre)
  3. Line of sight must be clear (no blocks in the way)

----------------------------------------------------------------
Panic radii (all verified against bytecode)
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Radius   Mobs
  -------  ----------------------------------------------------------
  8        zombie, husk, drowned, zombie villager, vex
  10       vindicator, zoglin
  12       evoker, illusioner, ravager
  15       pillager    <<-- largest coverage

----------------------------------------------------------------
Mobs that do NOT scare villagers (verified)
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Wither     - not present in ACCEPTABLE_DISTANCE_FROM_HOSTILES
  Frostbite  - not in the hostile list

  Many guides get these two wrong.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Line of sight (the most commonly missed condition)
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Even with glass, slabs or trapdoors between them, the sight check passes as
  long as the collision shape lets the ray through.

  That means:

    glass: does not block, fine to use
    slabs: half height, usually fine
    full blocks: block the ray, the villager cannot see the mob -> no panic

  WARNING This is the most common reason for "the mob is right there but the
  villager is not panicking".

----------------------------------------------------------------
Notes per mob
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Zombie
    Burns in daylight, must be shaded, or trapped in water / a boat
    Will attack villagers, needs separation

  Husk
    Does not burn, good for open-air farms
    Rarer than zombies (desert only)

  Drowned
    Does not burn underwater, good for underwater rooms
    Note it can also be pushed away by water streams

  Zombie villager
    Curing it creates positive gossip nearby, which may accidentally trigger
    the gossip path and skew your statistics

  Vex
    Flies through walls, cannot be caged
    Not a good long-term choice

  Vindicator
    High melee damage, will kill villagers outright
    Must be fully separated by blocks

  Evoker
    Summons vexes and fangs
    Extremely dangerous and hard to control, not recommended

  Illusioner
    Unobtainable in vanilla survival, command spawn only

  Ravager
    Tramples crops, heavy damage
    Cannot be contained in a boat, it walks out

  Pillager
    You MUST remove its crossbow first, or it kills villagers and golems
    Does not burn in daylight; best pick for 15 block coverage

  Zoglin
    Spawns only in the Nether
    Needs a portal or a spawn egg in the overworld


================================================================
5. Spawn point: where the golem appears
================================================================

  Search volume: 17 x 13 x 17 centred on the villager
                 horizontally +/- 8 blocks, vertically +/- 6 blocks
                 Source: the 8 6 arguments to SpawnUtil.trySpawnMob

  Attempts: up to 10 random positions per summon

  NOTE Before 1.19 (22w12a) the volume was 16x13x16.
       That change invalidated some spawn spots on older farms.

----------------------------------------------------------------
What makes a single position valid
----------------------------------------------------------------

  1. The block below must have a standable collision surface
  2. The golem's own body space (1.4 blocks) must be free
  3. There must be 2 blocks of headroom
  4. It must not be liquid or inside a wall

  WARNING The most common causes of "looks empty but nothing spawns":

     only 1.5 blocks of headroom (slab + half-slab stacking)
     trapdoors or fences underfoot (collision surface too low)
     the position being inside water (water is not a standable surface)


================================================================
6. Full decision flow (pseudocode)
================================================================

  every 100 ticks (5 seconds), for each villager:

    if the villager is not panicking:
        continue                    # only the initiator needs this

    if panicking villagers nearby is less than 3:
        continue

    # count participants
    count = 0
    for each villager v within 10 blocks (at most 5):
        if v is adult
           and v slept within 24000 ticks
           and v has no GOLEM_DETECTED_RECENTLY memory:
            count = count + 1

    if count is less than 3:
        continue

    # try to spawn
    for 10 attempts:
        pick a random position inside 17x13x17
        if the position is valid:
            spawn an iron golem
            break

7. Corrections: where common sources are wrong
================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------
Correction 1: there is no "no nearby villager summoned in 30s" condition
----------------------------------------------------------------

  WHAT IS CLAIMED
     "No nearby villager has summoned an iron golem in the last 30 seconds"
     is one of the spawn conditions.

  WHAT THE BYTECODE SAYS
     Villager only tracks two relevant memories:
       - LAST_SLEPT              (when it last slept)
       - GOLEM_DETECTED_RECENTLY (when it last saw a golem)

     There is no "RECENTLY_SUMMONED" or similar memory.

  WHERE THE MISREADING COMES FROM
     Most likely GOLEM_DETECTED_RECENTLY was read as "recently summoned".
     It means "saw a golem", not "summoned a golem".

  IMPACT
     Because this condition does not exist:

       a golem summoned by someone else still puts you on cooldown if you saw it
       but that is still "seeing", not "summoning"

----------------------------------------------------------------
Correction 2: the golem memory is 599 ticks, not 600
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Bytecode: setMemoryWithExpiry with 599L in GolemSensor

  Impact: implementing 600 flips the result at exactly the 30.00 second frame.
          The practical effect is tiny (1 tick = 0.05 s) but the number must be right.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Correction 3: participants do not need to be panicking
----------------------------------------------------------------

  WHAT IS CLAIMED
     Every villager participating in a summon must be panicking.

  WHAT THE BYTECODE SAYS
     VillagerPanicTrigger only counts panicking villagers to cross the
     threshold of 3, then calls wantsToSpawnGolem on nearby villagers,
     and that method only looks at sleep and golem memory.

  Impact: farm design gets much simpler.

          Only 3 villagers need to see the mob. The rest just need to have
          slept and not seen a golem.

----------------------------------------------------------------
Correction 4: since 1.16, profession and work state are irrelevant
----------------------------------------------------------------

  1.14.3 to 1.15: panicking villagers also had to work and sleep
  1.16 (20w19a) onward: no profession required, no recent-work requirement,
                        and no village bed count requirement

----------------------------------------------------------------
Correction 5: the wither and frostbite cannot scare villagers
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Many guides list the wither as a hostile mob.

  But WITHER is not in ACCEPTABLE_DISTANCE_FROM_HOSTILES, so it never causes
  panic.


================================================================
8. Practical checklist
================================================================

When a farm is not spawning, check in this order (most likely first):

  [ ] 1. Has the villager slept in the last 20 minutes?

         Shade the farm, or provide beds so they can actually lie down.
         A villager stuck in a boat or minecart can never sleep.

  [ ] 2. Are at least 3 villagers panicking?

         Use the skull icon in this mod: green means it is being scared.

  [ ] 3. Is the mob close enough?

         Zombie: within 8 blocks. Use the orange line in this mod:
         solid = working, dashed = too far, dotted = sight blocked.

  [ ] 4. Is line of sight clear?

         No full blocks in between.

  [ ] 5. Has the villager seen a golem recently?

         If a golem is stuck within 16 blocks for over 30 seconds, that
         villager stops spawning. Use the purple detection box in this mod:
         a golem inside the box means cooldown is active.

  [ ] 6. Is the spawn point valid?

         Use the small crosses inside the yellow box in this mod:
         no crosses means every position is invalid.

  [ ] 7. Are there 3 participants?

         At least 3 adult villagers within 10 blocks that have slept and have
         not seen a golem.


================================================================
9. Direct consequences for farm design
================================================================

  1. Golems must leave the 16 block radius quickly

     Otherwise that villager drops out of the pool for 30 seconds.
     This is the most common reason for "why is my farm rate so low".

  2. Only 3 villagers need to see the mob, the rest do not

     Participants do not need to panic.

  3. Beds are not strictly required, but sleeping is

     Since 1.16 the bed count does not matter, but the villager must really
     have slept within 20 minutes.

  4. A pillager covers 15 blocks

     This relaxes layout constraints a lot, but its crossbow must be removed.

  5. The spawn volume is 17x13x17 with 10 attempts

     An invalid position just burns one attempt, it does not fail the summon.
     But if all 10 are invalid, the attempt is wasted.
